I am currently working at a Robotics and AI startup.

Previously, I served as Head of R&D in the Family Robotics Department at NetEase Fuxi (2023–2024), working on robotics applications for everyday life. Prior to that, I was a Team Lead and Staff Algorithm Engineer in the Autonomous Driving Department at DAMO Academy, Alibaba Group (2021–2023), focusing on prediction, reinforcement learning (RL), and machine learning (ML) planning for autonomous driving.

From 2017 to 2021, I worked as a Research Scientist and Principal Investigator (PI) at the Institute of Infocomm Research, A\*STAR, where I also held joint appointments and led a team at the Institute of High Performance Computing and A\*STAR Artificial Intelligence and Data Analytics. During that time, I served as PI and Co-PI on multiple research and industrial projects, and supervised Ph.D., FYP, and intern students from local and overseas universities. Earlier in my career, I co-founded Beijing EwayBot and served as Chief Engineer (2015–2018), and worked as a Research Engineer at the Singapore Institute of Manufacturing Technology (2010–2012).

I received my B.Eng. degree with First Class Honours from Nanyang Technological University, Singapore, in 2010, and my M.S. and Ph.D. degrees from Carnegie Mellon University, Pittsburgh, PA, USA, in 2014 and 2017, respectively.

### Research Interests
* Robotics
* Reinforcement Learning & Machine Learning
* Task and Motion Planning
* Computer Vision
