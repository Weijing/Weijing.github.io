I am currently working at a robotics and AI startup.

Previously, I served as Head of R&D in the Family Robotics Department at NetEase Fuxi (2023–2024), where I led research and development on robotics applications for everyday life. Before joining NetEase, I was a Team Lead and Staff Algorithm Engineer in the Autonomous Driving Department at Alibaba Group's DAMO Academy (2021–2023), focusing on prediction, reinforcement learning (RL), and machine learning (ML)-based planning for autonomous driving.

From 2017 to 2021, I was a Research Scientist and Principal Investigator (PI) at the Institute of Infocomm Research, A\*STAR, and held joint appointments—including leading a team—at the Institute of High Performance Computing and A\*STAR Artificial Intelligence and Data Analytics. During this period, I served as PI and Co-PI on multiple research and industrial projects, and mentored Ph.D., FYP, and intern students from local and international universities. Earlier in my career, I co-founded Beijing EwayBot and served as Chief Engineer (2015–2018), and worked as a Research Engineer at the Singapore Institute of Manufacturing Technology (2010–2012).

I received my B.Eng. with First Class Honours from Nanyang Technological University, Singapore, in 2010, and my M.S. and Ph.D. from Carnegie Mellon University, Pittsburgh, PA, USA, in 2014 and 2017, respectively.

### Research Interests
* Robotics
* Reinforcement Learning & Machine Learning
* Task and Motion Planning
* Computer Vision
